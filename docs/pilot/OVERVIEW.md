# Ordixa pilot: order and payment reconciliation

**Less busywork. More clarity.**

*Draft for owner review. Bracketed items are filled in per customer.*

## What Ordixa does

Each period you export orders from your store and payments from your payment
processor, then check by hand which orders are paid, which are short, and
which payments do not line up. Ordixa does that check for you.

You send the exports. We map their columns once into a reusable import
profile, check every row, match each payment, refund, and adjustment to its
order using the order number both systems share, and send back one Excel
workbook that shows:

- totals for the period: order value, payments, refunds, net received, and
  outstanding balances;
- every order that is unpaid, partially paid, or overpaid, with a note on
  what to check;
- every row that could not be used, with its file, row number, and the
  reason.

Ordixa is a tool we run for you. There is no app to log into or install.

## Who it is for

Small businesses that already export orders and payments as CSV or Excel
files, use an order number that appears in both exports, and spend time each
month reconciling them by hand.

## What you send

- An orders export and a payments export for the agreed period, as CSV or
  `.xlsx` files. A customer list is optional.
- Only the columns we agree on during intake. Please remove card details,
  bank details, addresses, notes, tags, and anything else not on the agreed
  list before sending. Never send card numbers, bank account numbers,
  passwords, or government ID numbers.
- Files through [approved transfer method], not ordinary email.

## What you receive

- The exceptions workbook for the period, through the same approved method.
- A short note: the period covered, the headline totals, the orders that need
  follow-up, and any rows we could not use.
- Within [number] business days of receiving complete files.

## What happens when records do not match

Ordixa never guesses. A payment is matched to an order only when they share
the same order number in the files for the same batch. If a payment refers to
an order that is not in the batch, for example an order from last month, or a
mistyped order number, the payment is listed in the workbook as not matched,
with the reason. We ask you to confirm it rather than assigning it ourselves.
Corrected exports are processed as a new run; earlier results are kept for
reference, not overwritten.

## What the pilot does not cover

- Bookkeeping, tax, or accounting advice. The workbook is a review aid, not
  an accounting ledger.
- Payments for orders from earlier periods, unless those orders are included
  in the same batch. See [what happens when records do not match](#what-happens-when-records-do-not-match).
- Matching by amount, date, or customer name when order numbers differ.
- Direct connections to Shopify, Square, Stripe, QuickBooks, or other
  systems. We work from the files you export.
- More than one currency per profile, or currency conversion.
- Legacy `.xls`, tab-separated, or PDF exports.

## What a successful delivery looks like

- Every row in your files is accounted for: reconciled, listed with a
  warning, or listed as not used with a reason.
- The workbook totals agree with an independent check of your files.
- You can see in a few minutes which orders need follow-up and why.
- Your files are deleted on the agreed schedule, and we confirm it.
